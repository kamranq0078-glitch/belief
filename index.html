Your morning, before anything else

Not a routine. A ritual. There's a difference — one you follow, the other you protect.

  1. i
    water, before your phone

    One full glass. Your brain is 73% water and it's been fasting all night. You cannot think on empty.

  2. ii
    Rabbi zidni ilma, three times

    Say it out loud. Put your hand on your book. Mean it.

  3. iii
    name one thing you already know

    Before you open to what's new — remind yourself you are not empty. You've already filled pages.

  4. iv
    45 minutes, then breathe

    Not an hour. 45 minutes of complete focus, then 10 minutes of air and movement. Your brain consolidates memory in rest, not in more reading.

  5. v
    write by hand what you can't remember

    If you can't remember it, write it. Not type — write. The hand-to-brain loop is real. It will stick.

  6. vi
    end when you said you would

    A brain that knows when it stops learns to give everything until then. Don't blur your own boundaries.
